1. Executive Summary

BetGuard AI is a specialized real-time risk, compliance, and fraud-detection engine designed specifically for online sports betting and gaming operators. Sitting between front-end web/mobile interfaces and core Player Account Management (PAM) databases, BetGuard AI evaluates user telemetry, betting patterns, device fingerprints, and financial actions in under 150ms.

Core Value Deliverable: Reduces manual compliance review queues by up to 60% while preventing bonus abuse, multi-accounting syndicates, and account takeover (ATO) attempts prior to payout execution.

2. Operational Capability Matrix

Risk Vector Detection Mechanism Automated System Action
Multi-Accounting & Syndicates Device canvas fingerprinting, cross-account hash correlation Hold for Review
Account Takeover (ATO) Velocity anomaly checks, IP/ASN route switches, biometric shifts Challenge (2FA)
Bonus Abuse & Arbitrage Real-time wager-to-deposit ratio tracking via Redis Sorted Sets Reject Bonus
Standard Low-Risk Wagers Verified device hash, trusted IP profile, low composite risk score Auto Pass
